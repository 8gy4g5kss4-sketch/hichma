Our Story

Born from a Vision of Dignified Beauty

Hichma was founded in the heart of Marrakech by sisters Fatima and Khadija — two women who believed modest dressing should never mean settling for less.

The name Hichma — Arabic for modesty and grace — is our guiding principle. Every stitch, every fold, every choice of fabric is made with the intention of helping you feel powerful, elegant, and entirely yourself.

Today, we ship to women in 60+ countries. Each piece remains handcrafted by artisan families in Fez who have practiced their craft for generations.

— Fatima & Khadija
